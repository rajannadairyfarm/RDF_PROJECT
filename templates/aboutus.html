Who We Are

Nurturing Nature,
Nourishing You.

At Rajanna Dairy Farm, we believe that true health begins at the roots. Nestled in the heart of pristine pastures, our farm is a sanctuary where traditional values meet ethical farming.

By prioritizing the well-being of our indigenous cattle and sustaining the soil that feeds them, we ensure that every drop of milk reaching your home is as pure and untouched as nature intended.
