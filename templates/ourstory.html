Our Heritage

The Journey of Rajanna Dairy Farm

The Origins

A Shared Vision

It began with a simple, yet profound belief shared by two brothers, P Ramanakar and P RajKumar. Disheartened by the highly processed and artificial dairy products flooding the market, they envisioned a return to the roots—a place where milk is pure, untouched, and deeply connected to nature.

The Dedication

Cultivating Quality

Starting with just a handful of indigenous cows, the brothers poured their sweat and souls into the soil. Ramanakar focused on sustainable farming and animal welfare, ensuring every cow was fed organic, homegrown fodder. Meanwhile, RajKumar developed a meticulous system to bring this fresh goodness directly to local homes within hours of milking.

Present Day

The Rajanna Legacy

Today, Rajanna Dairy Farm stands as a beacon of purity and trust. What started as a small brotherly venture is now a family of thousands of happy customers who wake up to the authentic taste of real milk. The brothers' promise remains unbroken: Farm to Home, uncompromised.

Our Mission

"To bridge the gap between traditional, ethical dairy farming and modern households, ensuring every family has access to pure, untampered, and highly nutritious dairy products just as nature intended."

The Pillars

Meet The Founders

P Ramanakar

Co-Founder & Operations Lead

The heartbeat of the farm. Ramanakar oversees all agricultural and animal care operations. His deep knowledge of indigenous cattle breeds and organic farming practices ensures that our cows are healthy, happy, and yield the finest quality milk.

P RajKumar

Co-Founder & Distribution Lead

The bridge to your home. RajKumar revolutionized our supply chain, ensuring that milk reaches from our farm to your doorstep within hours. His focus on customer experience and uncompromising quality control keeps the Rajanna promise alive every single morning.
